Creamy Garlic Pasta with Parmesan

248 likes 1,840 views 32 comments
AP
Anya Patel
2,140 followers · 48 recipes
25
Prep (min)
15
Cook (min)
4
Servings
480
Calories

A rich, comforting pasta dish that comes together in under 30 minutes. The secret is using good quality parmesan and plenty of freshly cracked black pepper.

Ingredients
400g spaghetti
6 garlic cloves, minced
200ml heavy cream
100g parmesan, grated
3 tbsp olive oil
Salt & black pepper
Steps
1
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2
Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 2 minutes until fragrant but not browned.
3
Pour in heavy cream and simmer for 3–4 minutes. Add grated parmesan and stir until melted into a smooth sauce.
4
Add drained pasta to the pan and toss well. Add pasta water gradually until the sauce coats every strand. Season generously.
